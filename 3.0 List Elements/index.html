Krish's Cinnamon Roll Recipe

Welcome to Krish's kitchen! Here is my favorite recipe for delicious, soft, and sweet cinnamon rolls.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 25 minutes

Servings: 8 cinnamon rolls

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 2 teaspoons yeast
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg plus 1 egg yolk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 3 cups bread flour

For the filling:

  • 2/3 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the warm milk with yeast and sugar.
  2. Mix in the butter and flour until combined into a dough.
  3. Knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  4. Transfer the dough into a large bowl and cover it with plastic wrap. Leave it somewhere warm until the dough has doubled in size.
  5. After the dough has doubled in size, roll it out into a large rectangle.
  6. Melt the butter for the filling and mix in the sugar and cinnamon.
  7. Spread the filling onto the dough and roll the dough into a Swiss roll.
  8. Cut the roll into 3 cm sections and place them flat into a baking tray.
  9. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
  10. Bake the rolls for 20–25 minutes until lightly brown.

Enjoy Your Cinnamon Rolls!

Freshly baked cinnamon rolls are perfect to enjoy with a cup of coffee or tea. Happy baking!
