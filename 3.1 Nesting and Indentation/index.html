Krish's Cinnamon Roll Recipe

Ingredients

  • Dough
    • Warm milk
    • Yeast
    • Sugar
    • Egg
    • Butter
    • Flour
  • Filling
    • Brown sugar
    • Cinnamon
    • Butter

Instructions

  1. Prepare the dough
    1. Mix warm milk, yeast, and sugar.
    2. Add eggs, butter, and flour.
    3. Knead the dough.
  2. Prepare the filling
    1. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon.
    2. Spread butter over the dough.
    3. Add the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
  3. Bake the cinnamon rolls
    1. Roll and cut the dough.
    2. Place the rolls in a baking tray.
    3. Bake until golden brown.
